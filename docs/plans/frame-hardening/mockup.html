What the bar enforces

  • The frame's request carries only a destination. The viewer parses it with new URL and accepts http:, https: and mailto:. Credentials in the URL, relative URLs, and anything over 8 KiB are refused, and a refused request shows nothing.
  • The destination shown is the normalised address that will open, as literal text. Markup in it is text. The host is emphasised. An internationalised host is shown in punycode, as the browser will open it.
  • Open link is enabled one second after the bar appears, and only by a click the host receives as trusted. Cancel, Escape, a 30 second timeout, and a new render of the relic all close the bar.
  • One request is pending at a time. Requests that arrive while one is pending, or within two seconds of a close, are dropped and counted. The bar says how many.
  • The new tab is opened by the viewer with noopener,noreferrer. The frame never opens a tab.
  • The reader can tick Don't ask again for this relic before pressing Open link. The choice is recorded only by that trusted click, for this relic and this version, in the viewer's own storage, which the frame cannot read.
  • While the choice stands, a link requested during a reader gesture opens without the bar, its read delay and its cooldown. The strip above the frame offers Turn back on; it takes its own line of space and never covers the frame. A new version of the relic asks again, and a frame message can raise the bar but never sets or clears the choice.
