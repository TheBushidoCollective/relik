What the bar enforces

  • The frame's request carries only a destination. The viewer parses it with new URL and accepts http:, https: and mailto:. Credentials in the URL, relative URLs, and anything over 8 KiB are refused, and a refused request shows nothing.
  • The destination shown is the normalised address that will open, as literal text. Markup in it is text. The host is emphasised. An internationalised host is shown in punycode, as the browser will open it.
  • Open link is enabled one second after the bar appears, and only by a click the host receives as trusted. Cancel, Escape, a 30 second timeout, and a new render of the relic all close the bar.
  • One request is pending at a time. Requests that arrive while one is pending, or within two seconds of a close, are dropped and counted. The bar says how many.
  • The new tab is opened by the viewer with noopener,noreferrer. The frame never opens a tab.
  • The reader can tick Don't ask again for this relic before pressing Open link. The choice is recorded only by that trusted click, for this relic and this version, in the viewer's own storage, which the frame cannot read.
  • While the choice stands, a link requested during a reader gesture opens without the bar, its read delay and its cooldown. The strip above the frame offers Turn back on; it takes its own line of space and never covers the frame. A new version of the relic asks again, and a frame message can raise the bar but never sets or clears the choice.

    What changes on the other confirmations

    • Large file open keeps its dialog: filename, type and size, Cancel and Open. Don't ask again for this relic sits beside Open. Ticking the box records nothing. The choice is recorded only by a trusted click on Open, through confirm-prefs.ts as large-open, for this relic and this version. Cancel and Escape record nothing, and a new version asks again.
    • Delete comment keeps its dialog and its sentence. Don't ask again for my comments on this relic sits under the sentence. The choice is recorded only by a trusted click on Delete comment, as delete-comment, for this relic across its versions, because comments span versions.
    • While that choice stands, Delete on your own comment sends the delete at once and the thread shows the existing outcome line. Relik has no undo for it, so the strip above the thread says so and offers Ask again, and the Skipped confirmations list repeats the sentence the dialog used to show.
    • Skipped confirmations has two entry points. At 34rem and wider, where the actions sit in the bar, a Skipped button with the count appears only while at least one choice stands on this relic; at zero there is no button. Below 34rem it is an item in the More menu, with the count. Both open the same panel, which lists only this relic's choices, each with its scope in plain words and an Ask again button. Ask again clears that one choice, says so, and moves focus to the next row. After the last one the panel shows its empty state, and the bar button goes when the panel closes.
    • Private delete and private revoke are not kinds in confirm-prefs.ts, so nothing can skip them. Their sheets carry no checkbox and one line saying why they always ask.
    • A frame message cannot set, clear or read any of these choices, as with links.
